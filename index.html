What is an Interval? (Frog Hop Time!)

Imagine your guitar neck is a stream of stepping stones called Frets! 🪨

An Interval is just the distance a little frog leaps between two musical sound stones!

💡 Short Hop = Close Sound (1 or 2 frets)
🚀 Big Hop = Faraway Sound (5 or 7 frets)

The Secret Magic String! 🪄

Normally, when you hop down to the next string on a guitar, it counts as 5 frets distance. It's like taking a giant teleportation leap!

📏 Normal String Jump

5 Steps High!

Jumping from E string to A string, or A to D, or D to G always equals a 5-fret hop!

SPECIAL!
🦄 Magic G to B Jump

Only 4 Steps!

The G string to B string is sneaky! It only takes 4 fret hops to make the leap!

What is a Triad?

It's a 3-Scoop Ice Cream Sundae made of note intervals!

The word TRI means THREE (like a tricycle with 3 wheels 🚲)!

A Triad is a chord built with exactly 3 magic notes stacked on top of each other:

1️⃣ Root (1): The main flavor base note!
2️⃣ Third (3): The mood note (Happy 😊 or Sad 😢)!
3️⃣ Fifth (5): The strong superhero topping!
The 3-Scoop Triad Sundae
🍧 5th (Superhero Topping)
🍨 3rd (Happy/Sad Flavor)
🍦 1 (Root Base Scoop)

Unlocking Big Open Chords!

Why do 5 or 6 string chords only use 3 notes?

When you play a big chord like Open C Major or Open G Major, you strum 5 or 6 strings! But guess what? It's still just a 3-note Triad! Some notes are just TWINS (doubled) on higher strings! 👯‍♂️
